About Me

I am a mathematics graduate student at Binghamton University. I have received my Master's degree in 2022 and am awaiting the pending publication of my first paper. I specialize in topics such as:

  • Spectral geometry
  • The Weyl law
  • Pseudodifferential operators

I am casually interested in analytic number theory and the Riemann hypothesis, and in abstract harmonic analysis.

My PhD advisor is Professor Emmett Wyman.
